**Banking Customer Analytics Project**
---
**Overview**
---
This project presents a comprehensive customer analytics assessment conducted for a mid-to-large banking organization. The objective was to transform customer-level financial data into actionable business insights that support strategic decision-making across customer segmentation, risk management, and relationship performance.

The analysis focuses on understanding how deposits, lending exposure, and customer relationships contribute to overall portfolio performance, while identifying opportunities to improve profitability and mitigate financial risk.

**Business Context**
---
Financial institutions manage large volumes of customer data but often face challenges in converting this data into meaningful insights for leadership. Decision-makers require clear visibility into high-value customers, lending exposure, and the effectiveness of relationship strategies.

This project addresses those challenges by delivering a structured, insight-driven analysis aligned with real-world enterprise constraints and stakeholder expectations.

**Project Objectives**
---
Identify high-value customer segments contributing the majority of deposits

Assess lending exposure and loan-to-deposit risk at the customer level

Evaluate customer loyalty classifications against actual financial contribution

Measure advisor and relationship performance using objective metrics

Provide data-backed recommendations to support strategic planning

**Dataset Description**
---
The dataset represents customer-level banking information sourced from internal enterprise systems.

**Key attributes include:**

Customer demographics and identifiers

Bank deposits, savings, and checking balances

Business lending (loan exposure)

Loyalty classification and risk weighting

Banking relationship type and assigned investment advisor

**Scale:**

~3,000 customer records

25 structured attributes

#Tools & Technologies

Python – Data validation, aggregation, and KPI computation

SQL – Structured querying and segmentation analysis

Tableau – Interactive dashboards for executive review and exploration

**Analytical Approach**
---
The analysis followed a structured, business-first approach:

Data validation and preparation to ensure accuracy and consistency

Portfolio-level and segment-level aggregation of deposits and loans

Identification of high-value and high-risk customer segments

Evaluation of advisor and relationship performance

Development of executive dashboards to support decision-making

Technical complexity was intentionally abstracted to maintain focus on business outcomes.

**Key Insights**
---
Customer deposits are highly concentrated, with a limited segment contributing a disproportionate share of total assets

A subset of customers exhibits loan balances exceeding deposits, indicating elevated financial risk

Higher loyalty classifications generally align with greater asset holdings, though inconsistencies exist

Advisor performance varies significantly, presenting opportunities for optimization

**Business Impact**
---
The project provides leadership with clear, quantified insights into customer value, risk exposure, and relationship effectiveness. These insights enable:

Improved customer prioritization

Stronger risk governance

Better allocation of advisory resources

Data-driven portfolio management decisions

**Dashboards**
---
Interactive Tableau dashboards were developed to support exploratory analysis and executive reviews. These dashboards complement the formal report by enabling dynamic filtering, segmentation, and drill-down analysis.

(Dashboards are provided as separate artifacts and are not embedded within the report.)
